## English (en-US)

**App name** (11/30)
Tic-Tac-Toe

**Short description** (69/80)
Tic-tac-toe with an unbeatable AI, multiplayer, tournaments and stats

**Full description**

Think you can beat an AI that never loses?

Tic-Tac-Toe is the classic game you know, powered by an AI engine that can
calculate every game to the end. Pick the level that suits you, from a
relaxed match to the impossible challenge of beating a perfect AI.

FEATURES
• 4 difficulty levels, from Easy to Impossible (minimax AI with alpha-beta
  pruning)
• Play against the AI or with another person on the same device
• Tournament mode, best of 3 or 5 games
• Stats for games played, wins and draws, broken down by level against the AI
• Optional turn timer
• Undo move, against the AI too
• Your game in progress is saved automatically: pick up where you left off
• Light and dark theme
• Sound with volume control and mute
• Spanish and English, with automatic device language detection
• Accessible: full keyboard navigation, screen reader support, visible focus
  and respects "reduce motion"
• No connection needed to play

Free, no ads, no sign-up.
